Hourly Driver Booking – Questions & Answers

How do I hire a driver for a few hours in Gurgaon?

Visit LetsDrives.in or download the Let's Drive app, choose an hourly booking, enter your pickup location and time, and confirm. A verified driver is assigned to drive your car.

Will the driver drive my own car?

Yes. In hourly, weekly, outstation and monthly bookings, the Let's Drive driver drives your own car. If you also need a vehicle, see our transport and vehicle rental service.

Can I book a driver for an airport pickup or drop?

Yes. You can book a driver for pickups and drops at IGI Airport and for any local trip across Gurgaon and Delhi NCR.

Are Let's Drive drivers verified?

Yes. Every driver goes through identity verification and background checks before joining Let's Drive.
