Booking a Vintage Car for Your Wedding in Delhi NCR

A vintage classic makes for an unforgettable entry and stunning photos. Here's what to plan before you book one.

By the Let's Drive team · 10 November 2026

A gleaming vintage car for the groom's entry, the bride's arrival or the vidaai creates a moment guests remember – and photos you'll keep for life. Vintage cars need a little more planning than a regular car, though. Here's what to consider before booking a vintage or luxury car in Delhi NCR.

Vintage or luxury?

  • Vintage classics are perfect for short, slow, photogenic moments – the entry, the baraat or a photoshoot.
  • Modern luxury cars are better for longer transfers, airport pickups and the drive home after the vidaai.
  • Many couples book both: a vintage car for the entry and a luxury car for travel.

Plan the route and timing

  • Vintage cars are best over short distances – check the distance between the getting-ready venue and the wedding venue.
  • Confirm the venue entrance and driveway can fit the car.
  • Align timings with the baraat, band and photographer.
  • Keep a buffer for traffic and for photos before the entry.

Check before you pay

  • Ask for recent photos of the actual car you'll get.
  • Confirm it's chauffeur-driven and how many hours are included.
  • Ask about flower decoration and whether you can choose the theme.
  • For open-top cars, plan for heat, winter evenings or rain.
  • Ask what happens if the car has a breakdown – is there a backup?

Book early

Vintage and luxury cars are limited, and the best ones are booked weeks in advance during the wedding season. Lock in your date as soon as the venue is confirmed.
