Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This policy explains how Let's Drive ("we", "us") handles information on the website letsdrive.org.

Information we collect

This website does not have sign-up or payment forms. If you contact us by phone, WhatsApp or email, we receive the details you choose to share (such as your name, phone number, location and requirement) and use them only to respond to you and provide the service you ask for.

Bookings on LetsDrive.in and the app

Online bookings for drivers and valets are made on LetsDrive.in and the Let's Drive mobile app, which have their own privacy policy and terms.

Third-party services

This website loads fonts from Google Fonts, icons from cdnjs (Cloudflare), and an embedded map from Google Maps. Links open WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram, Google Play and the App Store. These services may process technical data such as your IP address under their own privacy policies.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the driver, valet or partner needed to fulfil your request, or when required by law.
